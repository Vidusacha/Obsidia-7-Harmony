# Obsidia-7 Harmony — Gameplay Mechanics & Physics Laws

## 1. Thermodynamic Resources
- **Charge ($E$)**: Electrical energy generated by `In-G` intake gas reactions. Flows through the body graph.
- **Materia ($M$)**: Physical scrap and mineral salts gathered by `In-M` claws. Required for synthesis of new blocks.
- **Information ($I$)**: Blueprint vectors and environmental signals received via `In-I` and transmitted via `Out-I`.

## 2. Kirchhoff Body-Graph Conductive Physics
The body is modeled as an electrical circuit graph $G = (V, E)$.
- **Conductance Matrix**: $L_{ij} = -g_{ij}$ for adjacent connected blocks, $L_{ii} = \sum g_{ik}$.
- **Node Potentials**: Solved via $L \cdot \vec{V} = \vec{I}_{\text{inj}}$, where $\vec{I}_{\text{inj}}$ are currents injected by `In-G` nodes.
- **Joule Heating ($I^2 R$)**: Heat generation at edge $(i, j)$ is $P_{ij} = (V_i - V_j)^2 \cdot g_{ij}$.
- **Overload Thermal Effect**: Excessive heat increases local block structural decay rate ($m$).

## 3. Maintenance Cost & Lever Stress
- **Base Power Maintenance**: 
  $$\text{Energy\_Loss} = \sum_{k \in \text{Blocks}} \text{Base\_Cost}_k \times \text{Stress\_Multiplier}$$
- **Mechanical Lever Stress**: Torque on a joint due to fluid/oil current drag:
  $$\tau_{\text{joint}} = \sum_{i} (\vec{r}_i \times \vec{F}_{\text{drag}, i})$$
  $$\text{Stress\_Multiplier} = 1.0 + \alpha \cdot |\tau_{\text{joint}}|$$

## 4. The Harmony Equation & Synthesis-Decay Dynamics
- **Dynamic Equilibrium**: Harmony occurs when synthesis rate $n$ equals decay rate $m$ ($n = m$).
- **Block Synthesis ($n$)**: Triggered when local Charge $E > E_{\text{threshold}}$ and Materia $M > M_{\text{threshold}}$.
- **Block Decay ($m$)**: Caused by base maintenance cost deficiency, thermal overload, or environmental damage (e.g. U.V. radiation, reactive gas G--).

## 5. Survival & Collapse Rules
- **Minimum Size**: Bodies with fewer than 5 blocks ($|V| < 5$) die and collapse into inert scrap junk.
- **Structural Catastrophe**: Loss of >50% of blocks in an organism leads to immediate reboot attempt. If Charge is insufficient to reboot within 3 environment cycles, the body disintegrates.
- **Resilience Standard**: Harmony target is achieving <2% body composition alteration when environmental parameters shift by 10%, and <7% alteration under 50% environmental shift.

## 6. Atmosphere & Gas Reactivity Scale
| Gas Type | Classification | Reactivity Rate | Charge Output | Risk / Thermal Hazard |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **G--** | Hyper-Aggressive | Instantaneous | Extremely High | Oxidation damage & severe thermal overload |
| **G-** | Active | Fast | High | Moderate heat generation; requires exhaust |
| **G0** | Balanced | Moderate | Medium | Safe & stable operation |
| **G+** | Inert / Lazy | Slow | Low | Requires catalytic energy input |
| **G++** | Fully Inert | Minimal | Very Low | Changes fluid density & buoyancy only |

## 7. Interaction Dynamics
- **Predation / Hunting**: Physical impact breaking structural joints to steal Materia & Charge.
- **Parasitism**: Docking onto a host organism's port to drain its Charge network.
- **Symbiosis**: Voluntary connection sharing specialized block output functions (e.g. one body provides `In-G` charge, another provides `Out-G` thrust).
